# Antislop audit 001 (2026-11-12)

Mode: AFTER (session override). Scope: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`.
Skills applied: antislop core, code, copywriting, human, layoutmobile, ui.

Nothing has been changed. Pick the numbers to fix; numbers not mentioned stay untouched.

## How this was checked

- Served the site locally and drove it in Chromium (Playwright) at 1440, 768, 375 and 320 px.
- The CDNs are blocked in this sandbox, so Pannellum 2.5.6 and Font Awesome 6.5.2 were loaded from the same npm versions. Google Fonts was not loaded (system fallback).
- Contrast: `contrast-check.py` on alpha-blended colors. Text over the photo was sampled from a screenshot at its lightest pixel.
- Product claims were checked against a clone of `efenow/tourbine` (`src/routes/dashboard.js`, `server.js`, `src/tour-data.js`, `src/csrf.js`).
- Click-through: stop chips open the matching hotspot, OS tabs switch panels, copy writes the right command to the clipboard, manual `<details>` opens, rotate button toggles its label. No console errors apart from the blocked font request.

## Checked and passing

- R-02: no em dashes in `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js`.
- R-17, R-36, C-5: every security and feature claim matches the source: bcrypt cost 12, 10 logins per 15 min, CSRF module, `httpOnly` + `sameSite: 'strict'` + `secure` in production, six media kinds, blur/mosaic editor, views over 24 h and 7 d.
- R-18, R-28: no testimonials. The FAQ questions are product-specific; only their framing is flagged (#17).
- R-24, R-26: every nav anchor has a matching section, and every control does something.
- R-32: skip link works; Tab order follows the visual order; `:focus-visible` outline on every control; tabs support arrow keys; hotspots open with Enter or Space.
- R-03: no horizontal scroll at 320, 375, 768 or 1440 px. The env table scrolls inside its own wrapper.
- R-27: if Pannellum fails to load, the static preview stays visible and the stop chips and controls stay hidden, so nothing is left dead.
- R-39: `lang`, title, landmarks, one H1, no zoom block; reduced motion stops the rotation, reveals and smooth scroll.
